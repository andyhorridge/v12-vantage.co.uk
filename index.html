The Car

Aston Martin V12 Vantage

The V12 Vantage combines Aston Martin's compact Vantage body with the company's naturally aspirated 6.0-litre V12.

With a six-speed manual gearbox, rear-wheel drive and carbon ceramic brakes, it represents one of the most distinctive driver's cars produced by Aston Martin.

This website records the history of this particular V12 Vantage from the day it joined the family in August 2015 through its later maintenance, upgrades and detailing.
